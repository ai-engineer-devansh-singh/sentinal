SENTINEL — How It Works

Multi-agent sepsis detection · from bedside data to clinician action
A patient wears a sensing patch that streams vital signs continuously. SENTINEL's AI council analyzes the stream in real time, catches sepsis before collapse, tells the clinician exactly what to do and why, and records every decision for governance. The system runs even when offline from the AI — the AI enriches the explanation; the structured data triggers the alert.

Step 4 · Deliberation
Orchestrator — The Council Reaches Consensus
A 3-stage deliberation: each agent states its case, they cross-examine each other, then a weighted consensus is reached. The AI (Grok) writes the plain-language explanation; the alert itself is always driven by the structured data.
Stage 1 · IndependentEach agent scores independently
Stage 2 · Cross-examAgents challenge each other
Stage 3 · SynthesisConsensus + action + uncertainty
Grok AI explains · data decides
